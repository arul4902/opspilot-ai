ENTERPRISE OPERATIONAL POLICY: LOGISTICS SLA & DELIVERY DELAY REMEDIATION
Document ID: POL-LOG-2026-v2
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: Shipping SLAs, transit tracking guarantees, and delay compensation.
================================================================================

1. FULFILLMENT & TRANSIT TIME SLAS
Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days are packaged and dispatched within 24 hours.
Standard Carrier SLAs:
- Ground / Economy Delivery: 3 to 5 business days from carrier scan.
- Priority Express Delivery: 1 to 2 business days guaranteed.
- Overnight Next-Day Delivery: Guaranteed delivery by 10:30 AM or end-of-day next business day.

2. DELAY IDENTIFICATION & COURIER EXCEPTIONS
A delivery is formally categorized as DELAYED when:
- Current calendar time exceeds the estimated delivery date (EDD) by 24 hours without an 'Out for Delivery' or 'Delivered' carrier event.
- Carrier telemetry streams an exception checkpoint such as: 'Weather Delay', 'Mechanical Breakdown', 'Road Closure', 'Sort Facility Exception', or 'Customs Hold'.

3. AUTOMATED CUSTOMER REMEDIATION & COURTESY CREDITS
When a customer contacts operations regarding a confirmed courier delay:
- Overdue by 1 to 2 Days: AI agent provides real-time GPS courier tracking link, explains carrier exception cause, and logs automated tracking monitor.
- Overdue by 3 or More Days: The order automatically qualifies for a $10.00 courtesy credit applied to their OpsPilot wallet, or a complimentary shipping fee refund.
- Overdue by 7 or More Days: The package is declared 'Lost in Transit'. The system offers the customer the choice between an immediate expedited free reshipment or a full 100% order refund.

4. SEVERE WEATHER & FORCE MAJEURE PROTOCOL
During severe weather alerts or national courier hub outages, carrier delivery guarantees may be suspended by the carrier network. 
However, OpsPilot customer care maintains courtesy credit issuance for customer satisfaction regardless of force majeure exemptions.
