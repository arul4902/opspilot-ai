================================================================================
ENTERPRISE OPERATIONAL POLICY: DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE & DEAD-ON-ARRIVAL (DOA) ITEMS
Document ID: POL-QA-2026-v2
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: Quality assurance claims, damaged shipping cartons, defective merchandise.
================================================================================

1. REPORTING WINDOW FOR DAMAGED SHIPMENTS
Customers who receive parcels with visible exterior damage, smashed cartons, broken security seals, or defective components must report the incident within 14 calendar days of verified carrier delivery.

2. EVIDENCE SUBMISSION PROTOCOL
To accelerate claim resolution without requiring postal fraud investigations:
- Photographic Evidence: Customer provides clear photographs of external shipping box, carrier label, and damaged product/serial number.
- Batch & Serial Inspection: Operations logs serial numbers in QA database to track potential manufacturing lot anomalies.

3. IMMEDIATE REMEDIATION OPTIONS
Upon receipt of damage confirmation:
- Replacement Fulfillment: An expedited replacement unit is dispatched at zero cost within 24 hours. The customer is supplied with a complimentary prepaid return label to return the damaged item within 30 days.
- Full Immediate Refund: If the customer declines a replacement or if inventory is depleted, a 100% full refund (including original shipping fees) is initiated.
- Return Waiver: For low-value items (under $25.00 retail) or glass items presenting a safety hazard, the operations agent may waive the physical return requirement entirely.
